Website and demo terms

These terms explain what the public website and fictional demonstration do—and do not—promise.

Last updated12 November 2026

1. About this website

This website describes an AI voice receptionist configuration and support service offered by Iftekhar Ahammed Samrat for UK plumbing and heating firms. It is aimed at business users, not household customers looking for a plumber.

Using the website or sending an enquiry does not create a service contract. Scope, price, included usage, setup, support, data handling and cancellation terms must be confirmed in a separate written agreement.

2. The SwiftPipe demonstration is fictional

SwiftPipe Plumbing & Heating is a fictional company created to demonstrate a working call flow. Names, service rules, postcode examples, booking windows and the £65 including VAT call-out charge belong to the demo scenario.

  • SwiftPipe is not presented as a customer.
  • The demo is not a testimonial or a claim of client results.
  • The demo rules will not automatically be suitable for another firm.
  • Any real deployment requires its own approved services, prices, areas and boundaries.

3. Not an emergency or professional advice service

This website is not a plumbing, gas-emergency or professional safety service. The gas sequence in the video demonstrates that a configured rule can stop normal booking. It is not a substitute for official emergency guidance, a Gas Safe registered engineer or emergency services.

No one should rely on the demo, website copy or an unapproved automated call flow when making a safety-critical decision.

4. No guaranteed commercial result

The service is intended to help a firm answer calls, capture information and apply agreed booking rules. It does not guarantee:

  • a particular number of leads, jobs or appointments;
  • revenue, profit, cost savings or recovered work;
  • that every caller will complete the conversation;
  • perfect transcription or interpretation of every accent, noise level or request; or
  • continuous availability of third-party phone, voice, calendar or automation services.

The value equation on the website is a way to compare cost with a firm’s own economics. It is not a forecast or performance promise.

5. Pricing and the managed pilot

No settled monthly price is published on this website. A pilot proposal should state the monthly fee, setup fee if any, included calls or minutes, extra-usage rate, support scope, change allowance, payment timing and cancellation process.

A price or term discussed informally is not binding until both sides accept the written proposal or service agreement.

6. Client responsibilities for a real deployment

A client remains responsible for approving the business rules and supplying accurate, current information, including:

  • services the receptionist may and may not handle;
  • service areas, availability, capacity and booking windows;
  • prices, VAT wording and other customer-facing terms;
  • safe escalation and emergency instructions reviewed by an appropriate professional;
  • access to authorised phone, calendar and record systems; and
  • lawful notices and instructions for call recording and customer data.

The client must review tests before launch and report changes that could make an existing rule inaccurate.

7. Third-party services

A configured system may use third-party voice, telephony, calendar, database and automation tools. Their availability and terms are outside the direct control of this website. The selected services, costs, permissions and data roles should be listed in the client agreement.

8. Website content and acceptable use

You may view and share a link to the website for legitimate business evaluation. You must not attempt to disrupt the site, misuse contact channels, impersonate another firm, probe connected systems or copy the demo and present it as your own client result.

Reasonable care is taken with the website, but public content may be updated and should not replace the written terms of an active service.
